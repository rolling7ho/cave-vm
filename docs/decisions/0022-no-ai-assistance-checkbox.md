# 0022: Contributors do not mark AI assistance, D49

Date: 2026-10-15 · Decided by: Ivan · Status: accepted

## Context

The design said AI-assisted contributions are allowed with disclosure in the PR, and the pull request template and `CONTRIBUTING.md`'s checklist asked every contributor to tick whether AI assistance was used. Ivan asked to remove that.

## Decision

- **D49.** The pull request template and `CONTRIBUTING.md` no longer ask contributors to mark AI assistance (Ivan). AI-assisted contributions stay welcome, and contributors stay responsible for everything they submit.

## Consequences

- The design's line on AI-assisted contributions drops "with disclosure in the PR".
- `AGENTS.md`, which tells agents to say so in their own PRs, is unchanged.
